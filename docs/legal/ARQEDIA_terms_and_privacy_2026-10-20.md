## 1. Acceptance of Terms

This Terms of Use and Privacy Policy ("Agreement") is entered into between you
and eBL Finance Inc., a Texas corporation doing business as ARQEDIA
("ARQEDIA," "we," "us"). By creating an account, accessing, or using the
ARQEDIA platform (the "Service"), you agree to be bound by this Agreement. If
you do not agree, do not use the Service.

This Agreement applies to three kinds of user:

- **Paid User** — an organisation's account on a paid plan, which adds the
  Paid User Terms in Section 4.
- **Recipient** — a person to whom a Trial or Paid User has shared a
  memorandum, who reads it through the Service and may register to keep

## 4. Paid User Terms

- **Plans.** Plans and their prices are shown at checkout and on the pricing
  page. Plans are billed monthly. Enterprise terms are agreed separately in
  writing.
- **Paddle.** Payments are processed by Paddle.com, which sells to you as
  merchant of record. Paddle calculates, charges and remits any applicable
  sales tax, VAT or GST, and issues your invoices. Paddle's own terms apply to
  the payment.
- **Renewal.** Subscriptions renew automatically each month until cancelled.
- **Credit.** Each paid month adds credit equal to the plan's monthly fee.
  Credit may also be bought in top-ups. Credit is spent on metered actions —
  filing a document, generating a memorandum, and shares beyond the plan's
  monthly allowance — at the prices shown in the Service before each action
  is taken. Credit is spent soonest-expiring first. Monthly credit expires at
  the end of the period it is granted for; top-up credit and refunded credit
  expire 30 days after they are added. Credit is not cash and cannot be
  withdrawn.
- **Charges.** Each metered action is priced and accepted before it is taken,
  and is charged once.
- **Refunds.** If filing a document fails because the document cannot be
  read, or generating a memorandum fails, the credit charged for it is
  returned to your wallet as credit. Deleting a document does not return its
  charge. Payments to Paddle are otherwise non-refundable except as required
  by law or stated in an order form.
- **Cancellation.** After cancelling, nothing further is charged, unexpired
  top-up credit remains spendable, and existing Content remains readable and
  downloadable.
- **Non-payment.** If a payment fails or a subscription is paused, filing and
  generating may draw only on purchased or refunded credit until payment is
  restored.

## 5. Recipients of shared memoranda

- **Reading.** A Recipient opens a shared memorandum with its link and a
  one-time code emailed to the address it was shared with. A Recipient may
  read it and download a copy, and nothing else.
- **Watermark.** Every page a Recipient sees or downloads carries their email
  address, the sharing Organisation's name and the time.
- **How long access lasts.** Access lasts 14 days from when the memorandum was
  shared. A Recipient who registers — a password, an authenticator app, and
  acceptance of this Agreement — keeps access for 6 months from when it was
  shared. A date set by the sharing Organisation overrides both and is never
  extended by registering. The sharing Organisation may revoke access at any
  time.
- **Marketing.** A Recipient may choose, when registering, to receive email
  about ARQEDIA. It is off unless chosen.
- **The sharing Organisation's Content.** A shared memorandum remains the
  sharing Organisation's Content. ARQEDIA keeps a record of each share and
  each view and download for the sharing Organisation's audit.
