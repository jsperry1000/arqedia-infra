# Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective 27 October 2026

## 1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy is part of the Agreement between you and Chase Creek LLC, a Delaware limited liability company doing business as ARQEDIA ("ARQEDIA," "we," "us"), described in the ARQEDIA Terms of Service. It explains what personal information ARQEDIA collects through the ARQEDIA platform (the "Service"), how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. Terms defined in the Terms of Service have the same meaning here.

It applies to Trial Users, Paid Users and Recipients.

## 2. Information We Collect

| Category | What |
| --- | --- |
| Account data | Name, email address, Organisation name and jurisdiction, role. Passwords are held by Amazon Cognito and never by ARQEDIA; an authenticator app may be added. |
| Sign-up data | The IP address a sign-up came from; for each sign-up attempt, the email domain, a one-way hash of the email address, the IP address and the outcome. |

| Browser storage | The Service uses no advertising or analytics cookies or trackers. It stores in your browser your sign-in session, a Recipient's verified-device identifier, and which notices you have dismissed. |

## 3. How Information Is Used and Shared

ARQEDIA uses the information above to provide and maintain the Service — storing your documents, reading facts from them, and composing memoranda and reports from those facts — and to process payments, authenticate accounts, prevent abuse of sign-up, provide support, and improve the Service. ARQEDIA does not sell personal information.

**Where it is held.** All Organisations share one platform, hosted by Amazon Web Services in the United States (Ohio, us-east-2). Each Organisation's Content is kept separate by the platform, identified as that Organisation's and encrypted; it is not held in a separate account per Organisation.

**ARQEDIA staff** can access Organisations' data to operate the Service, provide support and review abuse.

**Information may be shared with:**

- **Service providers** performing functions on ARQEDIA's behalf:
    - **Amazon Web Services**, which hosts the Service and its databases and storage, reads text from scanned documents (Amazon Textract), sends email (Amazon SES), manages sign-in (Amazon Cognito), and runs the AI models that read documents and compose memoranda (Amazon Bedrock), whose requests are processed in AWS regions in the United States and are not used to train the models;
    - **Paddle**, which processes payments as merchant of record.
- **Recipients** you choose to share a memorandum with.
- **Legal and compliance recipients**, where required by law or subpoena, or to protect ARQEDIA's rights.
- **Successors**, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, subject to this Agreement continuing to apply.

## 4. Data Security and Retention

ARQEDIA maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect Account and Content data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss: Content is encrypted at rest and in transit, and public access to storage is blocked. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and ARQEDIA cannot guarantee absolute security.

Account and Content data are retained for as long as the Organisation's account is active. A document you delete is removed from the Service; copies held in backups and earlier stored versions, including the facts read from it and the memoranda built from them, are deleted within 7 days. The wallet ledger is never altered or deleted, as an accounting record. Sign-up IP addresses are retained while the Organisation's account is active and deleted one year after it is cancelled. Records of shares, views and downloads are kept for 3 years for the sharing Organisation's audit.

## 5. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to: access the personal information ARQEDIA holds about you; correct inaccurate information; and delete your account and associated personal information. Deletion is carried out by ARQEDIA on request. You may download any memorandum as a PDF at any time. Requests may be sent to the contact in Section 7.
