# Refund Policy

Version 1 · Effective 27 October 2026

This Refund Policy forms part of the Agreement between you and Chase Creek LLC, a Delaware limited liability company doing business as ARQEDIA, described in the ARQEDIA Terms of Service. Terms defined in the Agreement have the same meaning here.

## 1. Who takes your payment

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, which sells to you as merchant of record. Paddle calculates, charges and remits any applicable sales tax, VAT or GST, and issues your invoices. Paddle's own terms apply to the payment.

## 2. What you pay for

Plans are billed monthly and renew automatically each month until cancelled. Each paid month adds Credit equal to the plan's monthly fee. Credit may also be bought in top-ups. Credit is spent on metered actions — filing a document, generating a memorandum, and shares beyond the plan's monthly allowance — at the prices shown in the Service before each action is taken. Each metered action is priced and accepted before it is taken, and is charged once.

## 3. When Credit is returned

The Credit charged for an action is returned to your wallet as Credit if the action fails: filing a document that cannot be read, reading the facts from a filed document, generating a memorandum, or updating a memorandum.

Returned Credit expires 30 days after it is added, and is spent soonest-expiring first, like all Credit.

## 4. When nothing is returned

- **Deleting a document** does not return the Credit charged for filing it.
- **Credit is not cash.** It cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money.
- **Payments to Paddle** are non-refundable, except as required by law or stated in an order form.
- **Changing to a smaller plan** part-way through a month returns no Credit to your wallet. Paddle may leave a credit on your Paddle account for the unused part of the month, which Paddle applies to your later payments.

## 5. Cancelling

After you cancel, nothing further is charged. The month in which you cancel is not refunded in part. Unexpired top-up Credit remains spendable, and your existing Content remains readable and downloadable.

## 6. If a payment fails

If a payment fails or a subscription is paused, filing and generating may draw only on purchased or refunded Credit until payment is restored.

## 7. The trial

A trial holds no card and takes no payment, so there is nothing to refund. When it ends, unused trial Credit expires.

## 8. Contact

Questions about a charge or this policy: info@arqedia.com.
