Somebody has shared a memorandum with you

You have been sent a link to a due diligence memorandum prepared in ARQEDIA. This page explains what the link is, what you can do with it, and what we know about you.

What the link opens

One memorandum, and nothing else. Not a folder, not an account, not the documents behind it.

You will not see the source documents, the extracted values, the sender’s configuration, or any other memorandum they hold. There is no way through the memorandum into their workspace, and there is nothing there for you to find.

The memorandum does not change while you hold it. If the sender needs to correct something they generate a new one, and that is a new decision to share.

Why you had to click a link in your own email

So that the sender can answer the question that matters in diligence: who read this. Opening the link verifies that you control the address it was sent to, and from that moment every open and every download is recorded against you — the sender can see when you first opened it, how many times you have returned, and whether you took a copy.

A link that anybody could forward would audit nothing, which is why this one does not work that way.

If you download it

You may download a copy, and it is yours to file — that is what the feature is for.

Every copy carries your email address, the sender’s name and the time you took it, written into the document itself rather than laid over it. If a copy of this memorandum turns up somewhere it should not be, that mark says which download it came from.

How long you have

Two weeks from the day it was sent.

After that the link stops working. The sender may set a shorter or longer date; if they have, theirs is the one that counts.

Registering, and what it buys

You can register on the address it was sent to. It is free, and it does one thing: your access runs for six months instead of two weeks — for this memorandum and for anything else shared with you afterwards, by anyone.

Registering means setting a password and a second factor, and accepting our terms and privacy policy.

Until you register we have no relationship with you: the sender gave us your address, we delivered a file to it, and we will write to you about this memorandum and nothing else. Once you register you have an account with us and can say whether you want to hear from us beyond that.

What the sender can do

They can revoke your access at any time, and it stops immediately.

Revoking does not recall a copy you have already downloaded. Nobody can. What it ends is your access here.
